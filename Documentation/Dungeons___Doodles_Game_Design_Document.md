### Game overview

Dungeons & Doodles is a small party game powered by AI. The goal of the game is to show that the use of AI in games does not have to be negative, its main purpose is simply to create a fun and social experience. In the game, players travel together through a dungeon inspired by Dungeons & Dragons. Along the way, the group encounters monsters. When an enemy appears, every player draws a weapon to fight it. After everyone finishes their drawing, the players vote for the weapon they like the most. The number of votes a drawing receives determines how much XP that player earns. Once the voting is complete, the players are shown an AI-generated story describing how the battle unfolds, based on the chosen weapons. Keeping the spirit of a party game in mind, the ultimate goal is to collect the most XP, earned through votes from other players and, of course, defeat the dungeon boss at the end.
